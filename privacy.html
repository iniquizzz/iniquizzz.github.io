Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This application ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own quiz videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

What we access

When the owner connects TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (display name) through TikTok Login Kit. The App uses this only to upload the owner's own videos.

What we store

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The App does not collect, store, or sell personal data of any other person.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties, other than sending the owner's own videos to TikTok through its official API.

Control

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions > Manage app permissions) or by disconnecting inside the App.
